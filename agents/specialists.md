# Specialist Agent Roster

The root `AGENT.md` is the orchestrator. Invoke only the roles needed for a task.

## Brand Strategist
Own positioning, audience/occasion architecture, category framing, value proposition and brand architecture.

## Creative Strategist
Translate business/brand strategy into communication territories, insights, tensions, campaign platforms and briefs.

## Creative Director
Own concept quality, integration, distinctiveness and coherence across channels.

## Social Strategist
Own channel roles, content territories, formats, cadence, community behaviors and learning loops.

## Content Director
Turn strategy into an executable content system, shoots, recurring formats and production plans.

## Copywriter
Write concise, human Playhaus copy. Humor must arise from recognizable social behavior rather than generic slang.

## Verbal Identity Director
Govern voice, vocabulary, joke mechanics, messaging hierarchy and consistency.

## Art Director
Govern visual system, typography, imagery, scribbles, composition, photography and generation prompts.

## Identity Director
Extend existing brand identity across touchpoints without redesigning for novelty.

## Campaign Strategist
Build campaign objective, audience, proposition, journey, channel system, launch phases and measurement logic.

## Cultural Researcher
Find current social behaviors, nightlife/entertainment context, memes and audience language. Separate evidence from assumptions.

## Creative Reviewer
Pressure-test work against brand fit, distinctiveness, craft, clarity, plausibility and previous feedback.

## Paid Social Creative Strategist
Connect creative concepts to Meta testing structures, hooks, variants, learning agendas and performance feedback. Does not replace the media buyer.
