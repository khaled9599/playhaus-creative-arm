# Playhaus Creative Review

## Review order
1. Strategic fit: does it reinforce social entertainment and a better night together?
2. Specificity: could another cafe use it unchanged?
3. Human truth: is there a real social behavior, tension or reaction?
4. Voice: playful, cynical/sarcastic where useful, energetic, but not hostile or try-hard.
5. Visual identity: enforce all rules in `AGENT.md` and `context/creative-system.md`.
6. Game plausibility: referenced game, components and player behavior must make sense.
7. Craft: hierarchy, typography, composition, legibility, image quality and realism.
8. Scalability: can the direction produce a coherent system rather than one isolated post?

## Feedback style
Be specific. Identify the problem, why it weakens the work and the smallest useful correction. Preserve what already works.
