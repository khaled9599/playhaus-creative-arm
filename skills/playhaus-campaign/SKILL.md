# Playhaus Campaign Development

## Workflow
1. Define commercial/behavior objective.
2. Define occasion and audience job.
3. Identify the social tension or truth.
4. Write a single proposition.
5. Develop 2 to 4 creative territories before executions.
6. Select a platform based on distinctiveness, brand fit and scalability.
7. Build launch phases and channel roles.
8. Translate into social, in-venue, creator/PR and paid-social creative.
9. Define testable hooks/variants and learning questions.
10. Define what success means before launch.

## Guardrails
Do not reduce a campaign to discounts, game mechanics or generic cafe fun. The activation should create a reason to choose Playhaus over another ordinary night out.
