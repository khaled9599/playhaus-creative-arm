# Game Night with Strangers

## Core concept
A recurring Playhaus night where guests can come alone and play board games with people they have not met before.

## Strategic role
Turn the Game Master and Playhaus format into a social facilitator. Reduce the awkwardness and friction of meeting people by giving strangers a structured reason to interact.

## Required workstreams
- Launch plan
- Campaign creative
- Organic social
- Reels/carousels/static content
- In-venue journey
- PR/creator angle
- Meta paid-social creative and testing
- Repeatable weekly operating format

## Creative principle
The story is not `meet strangers because networking is good`. The story is the social situations that games create: alliances, betrayal, competitiveness, accidental chemistry, awkward introductions becoming inside jokes and people staying for one more game.

## Guardrails
Keep it safe, welcoming and low-pressure. Do not imply dating-only intent. Avoid stock networking-event language.
